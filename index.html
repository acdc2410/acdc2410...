🎮 Play the Game

Aim with your mouse and left-click inside the game area to shoot.

Every 5 kills gives you a random special weapon: double damage, triple damage or 100 damage.

📖 Game Story

The Candy Kingdom was peaceful until strange monsters appeared from a magical portal. They want to steal all the enchanted sweets and destroy the wizard tower.

You play as a brave wizard who uses magical food missiles to defeat the enemies.

⭐ Game Levels

Level 1 👾

Aliens are slow and take 1 shot to defeat.

Level 2 👹

Food goblins are faster and take 2 shots to defeat.

Level 3 🐉

Dragon pasta monsters are strongest and take 3 shots to defeat.

🔫 Magical Weapons

🎮 Controls

  • Aim: Move your mouse inside the game area
  • Shoot: Left mouse click
  • Pause: P or Pause Button
  • Try Again: Try Again Button

💡 Tips to Win

Aim at enemies before they reach the bottom.

Every 5 kills gives you a special weapon for 5 shots.

The 100 damage weapon can destroy any enemy in one hit.
